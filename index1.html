The Three Foundations

Interconnected habits designed for sustained mental and physical resilience.

🌙

1. Quality Sleep

Restores neural pathways, clears cellular waste, and balances metabolic hormones. Adults require 7–9 hours nightly.

  • • Light sleep: Memory consolidation
  • • Deep sleep: Physical tissue repair
  • • REM: Emotional regulation
⚡

2. Regular Exercise

Builds cardiovascular strength, releases mood-boosting neurotransmitters, and prevents musculoskeletal decline.

  • • Target: 150+ mins aerobic weekly
  • • 2x weekly strength sessions
  • • Lowers risk of metabolic syndrome
🩺

3. Medical Check-Ups

Shifts focus from reactive treatment to early detection of silent conditions like hypertension or elevated glucose.

  • • Annual physical exam & blood panels
  • • Blood pressure monitoring
  • • Personalized preventive care
